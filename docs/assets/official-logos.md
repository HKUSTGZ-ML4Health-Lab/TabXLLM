# Official logo sources

These files are copied from the official websites without changing their artwork or colors. The page preserves each logo's aspect ratio and links to its organization.

- `neurips-logo.svg`: the NeurIPS logo used on the [NeurIPS 2026 official page](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026), downloaded from https://neurips.cc/static/core/img/NeurIPS-logo.svg. This is the conference's official logo; the year is displayed separately as text.
- `hkust-gz-logo.png`: the English university logo used on the [HKUST(GZ) About page](https://www.hkust-gz.edu.cn/about/), downloaded from https://www.hkust-gz.edu.cn/wp-content/themes/hkust-gz-official-0827/images/logo-e-blue-2x.png.

The logos belong to their respective organizations.
